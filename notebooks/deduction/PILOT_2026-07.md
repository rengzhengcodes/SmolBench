# Pilot study (July 2026 draft): findings and re-scored results

The July 2026 paper draft ran an earlier harness (`lean/` on branch
`deduction_lean`, LeanDojo backend, Benchmark 4 `fe4454af` corpus, 100
theorems, 10 model arms). A September 2026 review found scoring and analysis
defects; the harness was fixed, the stored responses were re-scored offline,
and the fixes were ported to this branch. This file records what changed,
what the re-scored numbers say, and what the pilot implies for the 21-model
study here. Tables are in `pilot_2026-07/`.

The re-scored rows (`lean/results/runs/main_v3_rescored`,
`main_v3_2_rescored`, 130 MB) are local to the pilot checkout. They are not
under `deduction_postcutoff/` on S3 because they were generated on the
pre-cutoff corpus; upload them under a separate prefix if they are needed
again.

## Design, in this branch's terms

| Paper level | Rung | Median prompt tokens (Table 1) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| None | `stepk:2` | 331 |
| MPI | `hint:0` | 366 |
| MPI+Signatures | `hint:1` | 489 |
| One-Hop | `hint:2` | 762 |
| Two-Hop | `hint:3` | 1387 |

`noise:N` is `hint:(N-1)` padded with filler to `hint:N`'s token count, so
`hint:N - noise:N` is the marginal effect of level N's content at matched
length. The pilot ran three rollouts per cell; the paired analyses below
use one analysis set per model, the (theorem, k) pairs with a scored row
at every level, and 95% paired bootstrap intervals over theorems.

## Defects found and fixed

Each item names the fix now on this branch.

1. **Figure levels were shifted by one.** Figures 5 to 7 labelled `hint:3`
   "One-Hop" and `hint:4` "Two-Hop", so the paper's level definitions and
   its figures disagreed. The "sweet spot then degradation" story in the
   draft's section 5.2 came from the off-paper 2-hop closure (median 3,592
   tokens). Fix: figure scripts read one level registry.

2. **Every newline was a separate tactic.** Multi-line tactics (indented
   `have` continuations, `induction ... with` arms, `calc`) were split and
   the fragments failed. Candidates with an indented continuation line
   scored 230 `lean_error` to 9 `success` (96%), against 19% for single-line
   candidates, and their share grows with context depth (9.9% at MPI, 12.8%
   at the deepest level), so the artifact leaned toward the paper's
   conclusion. Fix: `verify._split_tactics` groups continuation and `|`
   lines; the block is scored whole, and tactics after the goals close are a
   `lean_error`.

3. **Verdict taxonomy mixed model failures with infrastructure faults.**
   Lean timeouts raised and were recorded as `exception` (201 rows), then
   dropped from denominators and re-run on every resume; a dead REPL
   poisoned the rest of its block. Fix: `timeout` is a scored failure
   verdict, a killed REPL is reopened at the checkpoint, and `exception` /
   `replay_failed` are excluded from every denominator as missing data.
   Truncated completions (32,768-token cap; 35 rows, 28 with no tactic
   after 27k to 99k characters of reasoning) cluster in
   sonnet-4.6-thinking and kimi-k2.6-high at the None level. This branch
   keeps them as `no_answer`/`lean_error` and shows them in the `trunc`
   column of `analyze`; `max_tokens` in `sweep.yaml` is the knob.

4. **Theorem pool count.** The draft says 659 theorems remain after
   requiring a traced tactic; that is the count after an unmentioned
   `max_tactics: 5` filter (782 without it). The frontier subset is 30
   sampled theorems (15 in the analysis set), not 20.

5. **Noise padding was not length-matched.** The filler section header was
   not counted (every noise prompt ran 24 tokens over) and it told the
   model the block was filler. Provider tokenizers also count lorem ipsum
   7% to 12% higher than cl100k does. Fix in the pilot: the filler is fitted
   so the whole prompt matches, and the header carries no commentary. This
   branch already pads the whole prompt exactly, with whitespace units
   rather than filler text. The provider gap remains and is likely larger
   for whitespace runs, whose tokenization differs most between vocabularies:
   report provider-reported prompt tokens for `hint:N` and `noise:N` next to
   any hint-vs-noise figure.

6. **Per-rung trivial skipping.** Trivial cells are skipped per rung, not
   per theorem, so raw denominators differ across levels (None 94, MPI 63,
   MPI+Sig 61, One-Hop 61, Two-Hop 59). Fix: per-model analysis sets.

7. **"Full derivations" were a text scan.** One-Hop and Two-Hop followed
   every identifier in a premise's whole declaration (docstring,
   attributes, binder types) that named a globally unique corpus premise.
   Fix: `premises.referenced_premises` uses the LeanDojo trace when the
   premise's own proof was traced, and a namespace-aware resolver
   otherwise (`cli build-derivation-index`). Closure sizes barely move
   (1-hop mean 6.0 to 6.4, 2-hop 23.2 to 26.3); the change is
   definitional. 34 of the 100 sampled theorems have an empty MPI (final
   tactic is `simp`, `rfl`, `omega`, `exact h`) and drop out of every hint
   level; state that in the paper.

## What the pilot supports

- More positive information does not reduce pass rate for any model with
  n >= 48. DeepSeek and Gemini-high sit 14 to 23 points above MPI at One-Hop
  and Two-Hop with intervals excluding zero; Kimi-high is flat.
- Against matched-length filler, content clearly helps only at
  MPI+Signatures. At One-Hop and Two-Hop every open-model interval includes
  zero. Kimi-high at Two-Hop (-4.5 [-10.4, +1.2]) is the closest to harm.
- The open-vs-closed contrast cannot be assessed at n = 15.
- The surviving claim: the first step past the MPI (type signatures) adds
  value over filler; further derivational content adds little either way,
  so gains flatten while cost grows. That is narrower than "pollution".

## Hint ladder length

The closure has no token or premise cap (both removed 2026-09-21; the old
50k-token and 500-premise caps made `hint:7` and above byte-identical to
the level below for most cells). The text resolver accepts Unicode names
(`forall₂_congr`), unions a partial trace with text references for
term-mode proofs, resolves dotted heads (`ext_iff.symm`), and treats a
projection after a term (`(h).trans`) as context-only. The closure heading
is bare: no hop, premise or token count is shown to the model. Rendered on
the pilot's 63 analysis cells, cl100k prompt tokens:

| rung | hop | median | p90 | max | cells with no growth vs level below |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hint:3 | 1 | 1,226 | 2,836 | 7,736 | |
| hint:4 | 2 | 4,160 | 13,072 | 31,641 | 3 |
| hint:5 | 3 | 14,211 | 41,492 | 90,318 | 3 |
| hint:6 | 4 | 45,017 | 107,121 | 193,461 | 3 |
| hint:7 | 5 | 113,937 | 218,809 | 342,798 | 3 |
| hint:8 | 6 | 228,754 | 390,132 | 536,758 | 3 |
| hint:9 | 7 | 398,785 | 602,062 | 745,520 | 3 |

Growth is about threefold per hop. The three cells that stop growing have
closures that close under the corpus by 2-hop. Pick levels per model from
its context window (`hint:5` fits 128k at p90; `hint:6` needs 256k at p90;
`hint:7`+ needs 1M-class windows), and pair each `hint:N` with `noise:N`,
which costs the same tokens.

## Redesign: unflagged library block (2026-09-22)

Two weaknesses of the flagged ladder: the derivation side has strictly
more content than the MPI side (not just lower density), and the model is
always told which lemmas matter before it sees the block. The redesign
separates three factors and never marks the MPI:

| factor | rungs | what varies |
|---|---|---|
| flagging | `hint:1` vs `sig:0` | same lemmas, named at the top or buried unmarked |
| hops | `sig:0`, `sig:1`, `sig:2`, ... | the set: MPI lemmas plus N-hop closure |
| density | `sig:N` vs `proof:N` | the form: same set as signatures or as full proofs |

Controls: `signoise:N` (sig:0 padded to sig:N) isolates hop content from
hop length; `proofnoise:N` (sig:N padded to proof:N) isolates proof bodies
from their length. The block is sorted in import order then line, so
every dependency precedes its dependents and the MPI lemmas sit wherever
Mathlib puts them, unmarked. A shuffled-block variant is the robustness
check for position.

Example, `BilinForm.dualBasis_dualBasis` at k=1 (cl100k tokens):

| rung | tokens | entries |
|---|---|---|
| hint:1 | 599 | 2 flagged |
| sig:0 | 604 | 2 |
| sig:1 | 900 | 7 |
| sig:2 | 1,656 | 17 |
| proof:0 | 628 | 2 |
| proof:1 | 1,035 | 7 |
| proof:2 | 2,556 | 17 |

The comparisons that carry the thesis, in order: `hint:1` vs `sig:0`
(cost of finding the needle among true facts); `sig:N` vs `signoise:N`
(does more true content past the MPI help, hurt, or match blank length);
`proof:N` vs `proofnoise:N` (does the same set at lower density cost
anything). `hint_vs_noise.py --info-rung sig:2 --noise-rung signoise:2`
scores any of these pairs.

## Implications for the 21-model study

- `sweep.yaml` runs `stepk:1, hint:2, noise:3, hint:3`. That pairs Two-Hop
  with its control but cannot show where the ladder flattens. The pilot's
  only clear content effect was at `hint:1`; add `hint:0`, `hint:1`,
  `noise:1`, `noise:2` (and `stepk:2` for the None baseline the paper
  reports) if the flattening claim is to be tested here.
- `hint_vs_noise.py --info-rung hint:1 --noise-rung noise:1` (and `hint:2`
  / `noise:2`) then gives the per-level marginal directly.
- Run `cli build-derivation-index` on the post-cutoff corpus before any
  `hint:3` sweep so the closure follows traced usage.
- Keep `max_tokens` high enough that reasoning models do not truncate at
  the None level; check the `trunc` column on the smoke run before the
  full sweep. The pilot's uncapped reasoning lengths did not correlate
  with prompt length (Spearman rho about -0.05), so a larger budget costs
  little at the deeper rungs.
